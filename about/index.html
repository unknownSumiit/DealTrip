About DealTrip

Useful savings information, without hiding the fine print.

DealTrip is an independent U.S.-focused coupon and discount discovery website. We place special emphasis on age-based offers because eligibility varies widely between merchants.

Our mission

Make it easier to understand whether a deal applies to you before you click through to a merchant.

Our approach

Show source links, verification dates, meaningful restrictions and exact age requirements whenever they are available.

Independent from the merchants we list

DealTrip is not owned by, operated by or automatically affiliated with the merchants named on this website. Trademarks and brand names are used for identification and belong to their respective owners.

Revenue

DealTrip may use advertising or affiliate links. If an outbound link is an affiliate link, we may earn a commission if you complete a qualifying transaction. This does not increase the merchant's stated price because of DealTrip, although the merchant controls its own pricing and terms.

Who we serve

Our content is written for consumers in the United States. Some linked merchant offers may have different rules outside the U.S.
