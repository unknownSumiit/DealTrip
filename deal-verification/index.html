Editorial standard

How we verify deals

Our goal is to publish fewer, clearer listings rather than copy large coupon lists that may be outdated.

Starter-data standard: The initial deals bundled with this site were checked against official merchant pages on September 26, 2026.

What we check

  • The merchant or brand offering the discount.
  • The minimum age or other eligibility rule, when applicable.
  • Whether the offer is online, in-store, location-specific or capacity-controlled.
  • Material restrictions that could affect whether a shopper can use the offer.
  • A direct merchant source that users can open to confirm current terms.

What “verified” means

Verified means DealTrip reviewed the stated offer against the linked source on the date shown. It does not guarantee that the offer will still be available when you visit the merchant, because merchants can change offers at any time.

What we avoid

  • Invented coupon codes.
  • Claiming a percentage discount when the merchant only offers variable pricing.
  • Calling every age-based offer “50+” without showing the actual age threshold.
  • Implying DealTrip is affiliated with a merchant when it is not.

Corrections

Send corrections to hello@dealtrip.com. Include the merchant, the DealTrip page and, if possible, a link to the merchant's updated terms.
