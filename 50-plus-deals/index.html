How to use age-based discounts

Age-based offers often require identification, account verification, a membership program, a specific shopping day or a qualifying rate code. Read the merchant's current terms before traveling or making a purchase.

Why DealTrip uses exact age labels

The words “senior discount” can hide important differences. A 55+ retail discount and a 65+ rail discount are not interchangeable. We display the threshold stated by the merchant and link to the source we reviewed.
