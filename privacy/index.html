Privacy Policy

Effective: September 26, 2026

Current starter site: The version supplied in this package does not include Google Analytics, advertising pixels, account registration or a server-side contact form. If you add those technologies, update this policy and implement any legally required consent controls before deploying them.

1. Information you choose to provide

If you email DealTrip, we receive the information you include in your message, such as your email address, name and the content of your request. We use it to respond, investigate corrections, manage partnerships or protect the website.

2. Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider may process standard technical request information needed to deliver and secure the website, such as IP address, browser details, request time and requested URL. DealTrip's starter front end does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies.

3. Outbound merchant links

When you follow a link to another website, that merchant or third party operates under its own privacy policy. DealTrip does not control the merchant's cookies, accounts, checkout or data practices.

4. Affiliate links

Some links may contain affiliate or referral parameters. An affiliate network or merchant may use those parameters and related technologies to attribute a qualifying transaction. Review the privacy notice of the merchant or affiliate service involved.

5. Advertising and analytics

DealTrip may add analytics or advertising services in the future. Before activating technologies that collect personal data or use cookies for advertising, we will update this policy and provide notices or consent mechanisms where required.

6. How we use information

  • Operate, maintain and secure DealTrip.
  • Respond to support, correction and partnership messages.
  • Understand and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect rights, safety and integrity.

7. Data retention

We retain email correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was received, legitimate business records, dispute resolution, security or legal obligations.

8. U.S. state privacy rights

Depending on where you live and the data practices active on DealTrip at the time, applicable state law may give you rights relating to personal information. You may contact us to ask about a privacy request. We will evaluate requests under the law that applies to our actual data processing.

9. Children's privacy

DealTrip is intended for a general adult shopping audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children under 13 to provide personal information through the starter site.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to a lightweight informational website, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website, vendors or legal obligations change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

12. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@dealtrip.com.
