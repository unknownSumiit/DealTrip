How DealTrip works

We organize deal information, show important eligibility details and send you to the merchant to confirm and redeem the offer.

Find a deal

Search by merchant, category or age. Age-based listings display the minimum age published by the merchant.

Read the terms

Review location, eligibility, restrictions and our last-check date before opening the merchant page.

Confirm with the merchant

Use the official offer page as the final source of truth before you purchase, travel or visit a store.

DealTrip is an independent discovery website

DealTrip is not the merchant and does not issue the discounts shown on the site. Brand names belong to their respective owners. A merchant listing does not imply sponsorship, endorsement or partnership unless we explicitly state otherwise.

Why a deal may change

Merchants can change prices, exclusions, participating locations, age rules and availability without notifying DealTrip. That is why each listing includes a review date and a link to the merchant's own page.

Reporting an issue

If a deal is no longer available or its terms have changed, email hello@dealtrip.com with the merchant name and the page you were viewing.
