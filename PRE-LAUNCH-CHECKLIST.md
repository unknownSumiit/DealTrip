# DealTrip pre-launch checklist

## Required business details to confirm
- [ ] Domain is correct (`dealtrip.com` used in starter files)
- [ ] `hello@dealtrip.com` is a working inbox
- [ ] Business/advertiser name used in Google Ads matches your verified business/domain identity
- [ ] No false merchant affiliation or endorsement language

## Content
- [ ] Every promoted offer is live and easy to find on the landing page
- [ ] Every percentage/price/age claim matches the merchant source
- [ ] Expired offers removed promptly
- [ ] Merchant terms rechecked before ad launch
- [ ] Affiliate or sponsored relationship disclosed when applicable

## Technical
- [ ] HTTPS works on custom domain
- [ ] No 404 or redirect loops on ad landing pages
- [ ] Site works on mobile and desktop
- [ ] Google AdsBot is not blocked
- [ ] `robots.txt` is reachable
- [ ] Privacy, Terms, About and Contact pages are reachable from footer
- [ ] No forced pop-ups or automatic downloads

## Privacy
- [ ] Privacy Policy matches actual analytics, ad tech and form collection
- [ ] Consent management added if required for the data practices and users you target
