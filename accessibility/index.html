Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Updated: September 26, 2026

DealTrip aims to provide a website that is usable by a broad range of people and assistive technologies.

What we have built into the site

  • Semantic headings and landmark regions.
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and controls.
  • A visible skip-to-content link when focused.
  • Responsive layouts that work at narrow viewport widths.
  • Text labels for important controls rather than relying only on color.
  • Reduced-motion support for users who request it.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility problem, email hello@dealtrip.com. Please include the page URL, the issue you experienced and, if you are comfortable sharing it, the browser or assistive technology you were using.

Third-party websites

Merchant websites linked from DealTrip are operated independently. We cannot control the accessibility of those external websites, but we welcome reports when an outbound link creates a barrier.
